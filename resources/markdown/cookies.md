# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: 2025-09-24_

We use cookies and similar technologies to run the site and understand usage.

## What Are Cookies?
Small text files stored on your device by your browser.

## Types We Use
| Type | Purpose | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strictly Necessary | Core features like login/session and CSRF protection. | `XSRF-TOKEN`, `laravel_session` |
| Preferences | Remember choices like UI settings. | e.g., theme or locale flags |
| Analytics | Understand site usage to improve performance. | aggregate page views, error rates |
| Functional | Optional features that enhance experience. | editor or embed settings |

> If you select “Remember me” at login, Laravel may set a persistent cookie (e.g. `remember_web_*`) on your device.

## Managing Cookies
Most browsers let you remove or block cookies in settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break core functionality.
