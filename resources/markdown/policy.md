# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2025-09-24_

This explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

- Secure accounts and prevent abuse.
- Communicate about updates and service notices.

## International Transfers

- Data may be processed outside your country with appropriate protections.
